How is this 100% free?

1

Your browser is the server

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages — free. It talks directly to the model over Hugging Face's public Gradio API. There is no backend, no subscription, no credit-card wall between you and the GPU.

2

ZeroGPU donates the compute

Hugging Face's ZeroGPU pool lends spare H200 time to public Spaces like Wan2.2 14B Fast. Queues can form at peak hours — that's the only "cost": a little patience.

3

The engine is absurdly optimized

Wan 2.2 14B image-to-video, distilled with the Lightning LoRA to 4–8 denoising steps, FP8-quantized and ahead-of-time compiled (AoT) — so a clip that once took minutes now renders in seconds of GPU time.

4

Your data stays yours

Images are resized in your browser and sent straight to the model; videos come back and are cached only in your own browser's storage. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking pixels.

Engine: zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster · Weights: Wan-AI/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B (Apache-2.0) · LoRA: Wan22-Lightning. Reylo AI is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with Hugging Face or the model authors.

Questions, answered

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Really free. The model runs on Hugging Face's free ZeroGPU quota for public Spaces. The catch is purely practical: at busy times you wait in a shared queue, and very long abuse sessions can hit a temporary quota cooldown. Wait a few minutes and it's back.

Why does my first video take longer?

The Space sleeps when idle. The first request of a while triggers a cold start (1–2 minutes) before the GPU begins denoising. Reylo AI retries automatically and shows live status while you wait.

Text-to-video? Longer videos? HD?

This engine is image-to-video: it animates a frame you give it (which is why results stay coherent and beautiful). Clips run up to 5 seconds at 16 fps, 832×480. For text-to-video, generate a still first with any free image model, then animate it here.

Who owns the videos I make?

You do — the Wan 2.2 weights are Apache-2.0 licensed. Please still respect the law and people's rights: don't animate images you have no right to use.

Where are my videos stored?

In your own browser (IndexedDB), never on our side — this site has no server at all. Clearing browser storage clears the gallery; use Download to keep a file.

I hit a quota / auth error. Now what?

Two free fixes: wait a few minutes for the shared pool to free up, or paste your own free Hugging Face token in Advanced settings so the engine uses your personal ZeroGPU quota instead of the anonymous one. The token never leaves your browser except as an Authorization header to Hugging Face.
